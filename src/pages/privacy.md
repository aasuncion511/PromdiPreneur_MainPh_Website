_Last updated: 30 October 2026_

This notice explains, in plain English, what the PromdiPreneur website collects and why. It was written with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) in mind. For any privacy question or request, email **admin@promdipreneur.com**.

## Who we are

PromdiPreneur is a business systems and automation practice based in Cagayan de Oro, Philippines.

## What we collect when you send a question

When you use the "Ask a question" form we collect your name, email, optional business name, your message, how you prefer we reply, and your agreement to be contacted. We also record how you found us (campaign tags such as `utm_source=facebook`, the page you landed on and the referring website), a one-way scrambled code made from your internet address (to limit spam), and your browser type.

Please don't send sensitive information such as government ID numbers, health details or bank details.

## The free business guide

When requests for the free guide are open, the form collects your first name, email, type of business and optional business name, plus your agreement to receive the guide and a few follow-up tips by email. You can unsubscribe from those emails at any time. **The guide form is not active yet; nothing is collected from it until it opens.**

## How we use it

Only to answer your inquiry or send the guide you asked for, to follow up on that same conversation, and to understand which channels bring people to us. We don't sell your information. We never treat permission for email as permission for SMS, calls or WhatsApp.

## Where it is kept and who can see it

Inquiries are stored privately with our website host (Netlify) and a notification copy is emailed to admin@promdipreneur.com through our email provider (Resend). Contact details may also be saved in our customer management system (GoHighLevel) so we can follow up properly. These providers may process data outside the Philippines. Only PromdiPreneur can access this information; it is never publicly visible.

We keep inquiries for up to 12 months after our last conversation, then delete them, unless you become a client and the information is needed for that work.

## Analytics and cookies

If you choose **Allow** in the analytics prompt, we count page visits and clicks (for example on "Visit Digital Shop") using Google Analytics and/or Umami. We never send your name, email or message to analytics, and Google signals and ad personalization are turned off. If you choose **No thanks**, or never choose, no analytics script is loaded. You can change your choice with "Analytics settings" at the bottom of any page.

The site also keeps two small items in your own browser: your analytics choice, and, for this visit only, the campaign tags you arrived with (so we can pass them to our product pages).

## Our product pages and other sites

Our Digital Shop, Cleaning Business Kits and Payroll pages, our Facebook page and our international portfolio are separate websites with their own terms. When you click through, we may add the campaign tags you arrived with to the link.

## Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the information you sent, or to stop contacting you. Email admin@promdipreneur.com and we will act on it. You may also raise concerns with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).
